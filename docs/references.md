# References and what they support

The bibliography uses publication issue years; online dates are added when relevant. Long author lists use first author followed by “et al.” The [reference audit](reference-audit.json) records source-by-source access and limitations; the [literature review](literature-review.md) preserves the bounded discovery history.

## Research articles

- **Sarkisyan KS et al. (2016).** Local fitness landscape of the green fluorescent protein. *Nature* **533**(7603):397–401. [DOI: 10.1038/nature17995](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature17995). [PMID: 27193686](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27193686/). Original measured sequence–phenotype landscape and biological context; this repository does not reproduce its fitted model.
- **Rao R et al. (2019).** Evaluating Protein Transfer Learning with TAPE. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems* **32**. [Official proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/37f65c068b7723cd7809ee2d31d7861c-Abstract.html); [arXiv:1906.08230](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.08230). TAPE means Tasks Assessing Protein Embeddings. It supplies sequence/task context; our subset and models define a different evaluation.
- **Greenman KP, Amini AP, Yang KK (2025).** Benchmarking uncertainty quantification for protein engineering. *PLOS Computational Biology* **21**(1):e1012639. [DOI: 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012639](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012639). [PMID: 39775201](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39775201/). Evidence that uncertainty quality and acquisition outcomes depend on task and evaluation. Its numerical findings are not estimates for this GFP heuristic.
- **Balleza E, Kim JM, Cluzel P (2018).** Systematic characterization of maturation time of fluorescent proteins in living cells. *Nature Methods* **15**(1):47–51. [DOI: 10.1038/nmeth.4509](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.4509). [PMID: 29320486](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29320486/). Published online 20 November 2017. Supports maturation as a contributor to reporter interpretation; its experimental effects are not transferred to this dataset.
- **Parkinson J, Wang W (2023).** Linear-Scaling Kernels for Protein Sequences and Small Molecules Outperform Deep Learning While Providing Uncertainty Quantitation and Improved Interpretability. *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling* **63**(15):4589–4601. [DOI: 10.1021/acs.jcim.3c00601](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.3c00601). [PMID: 37498239](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37498239/). An abstract-screened alternative method, not implemented or reproduced here; the title is not a claim that it would outperform this benchmark.
- **Luo X et al. (2025).** Benchmarking recent computational tools for DNA-binding protein identification. *Briefings in Bioinformatics* **26**(1):bbae634. [DOI: 10.1093/bib/bbae634](https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbae634). [PMID: 39657630](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39657630/). First published online 22 November 2024. Supports the importance of leakage-aware evaluation in a different protein task; no result is transferred quantitatively.

## Data and software

- **Bolotin D, archive depositor (2016).** *Local fitness landscape of the green fluorescent protein*. Figshare dataset, version 1. [DOI: 10.6084/m9.figshare.3102154.v1](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.3102154.v1). Original files and phenotype definitions; CC BY 4.0. Accessed 20 October 2026. Cite Sarkisyan et al. as the associated research study.
- **TAPE maintainers.** [Official repository README](https://github.com/songlab-cal/tape). Raw fluorescence derivative and original-paper versus later-code distinction. Accessed 20 October 2026; observed file identity is in the audit.
- **Protein UQ maintainers.** [Official repository](https://github.com/microsoft/protein-uq). Reproduction workflow context. Accessed 20 October 2026; source code was not executed or copied. The historical rapid review records its own pinned inspection.
- **scikit-learn developers.** [RandomForestRegressor](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/generated/sklearn.ensemble.RandomForestRegressor.html) and [regression metric guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/1.8/modules/model_evaluation.html#regression-metrics), version 1.8.0. Official documentation, accessed 20 October 2026. Supports estimator and arithmetic definitions, not calibrated biological uncertainty.
- **SciPy developers.** [spearmanr](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.17.0/reference/generated/scipy.stats.spearmanr.html), version 1.17.0. Official documentation, accessed 20 October 2026. Supports rank-association and constant-input interpretation; no p-value inference is used.
