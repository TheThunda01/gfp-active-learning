# Assumptions and design choices

The purpose is to compare learning from a fixed measured pool, not to claim that the simulation recreates a laboratory. A rationale explains a choice; it is not evidence that the choice is optimal. These statements apply to the original benchmark and the four-policy ablation unless stated otherwise.

| Assumption or choice | Rationale and evidence | What is actually checked | Consequence if it fails or changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The archived median log-brightness is the target to learn. | The original experiment and dataset define it ([Sarkisyan et al., 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature17995)). Keeping it avoids silently substituting a different endpoint. | Source matching, rounding tolerance and input hashes; no remeasurement. | Errors describe agreement with this archived phenotype, not intrinsic molecular brightness or measurement truth. |
| Every stored pool label can be revealed once at equal cost. | A pragmatic simulation assumption that enables equal-budget comparisons. The archive contains measured survivors; it does not justify universal assay success or equal real costs. | No duplicate queries; exact budgets; only acquired labels are returned by the oracle interface. | Differential costs, failed assays and changed conditions could change policy rankings in practice. |
| Full-length 237-residue canonical strings are comparable position by position. | This bounded subset permits aligned Hamming distance without an inferred alignment; shorter, stop-containing records are excluded explicitly. | Length/alphabet checks and unique original-record matching. | The analysis does not cover truncations, insertions, deletions or excluded observations. Selection may narrow representativeness. |
| 2,400 pool, 400 local and 800 distant rows are a useful small demonstration. | These are pragmatic compute/coverage choices, chosen by label-independent salted hashes. TAPE supplies source split context ([Rao et al., 2019](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/37f65c068b7723cd7809ee2d31d7861c-Abstract.html)), not support for these exact sizes. | Role counts, unique sequence hashes and actual nearest-pool distances. | One subset cannot estimate variability across subsets or across protein families. |
| Reference substitution count separates two evaluation regimes. | It preserves the source task’s near/far distinction and asks a limited distribution-shift question. | Pool/local ≤3 substitutions; distant ≥4; between-set distances reported separately. | “Distant” does not imply unrelated sequences or a guaranteed minimum distance from training. |
| Composition plus substitution count is a useful baseline representation. | It is inexpensive and interpretable; it is deliberately incomplete rather than a mechanistic account of fluorescence. | Feature arithmetic; no phenotype-derived features. | Position-specific effects, interactions and distinct sequences sharing descriptors can cause error. No ablation here proves representation sufficiency. |
| A 64-tree forest, depth 10, minimum leaf 2, and fixed seeds are adequate for a bounded comparison. | Pragmatic runtime and complexity settings; all policies use the same model. The forest API documents behavior, not an optimal configuration. | Recorded settings, fixed seed rule and deterministic reproduction. | Conclusions are conditional on these choices. A different model may change which acquisition policy helps. |
| Starting with 64 labels and reporting budgets 64/128/192/256/384 is informative. | A pragmatic schedule covering several increasing label budgets within the small pool; it is not derived from laboratory cost evidence. | Matched starting sets, exact budgets and no repeated acquisitions. | Different starts, batch sizes or stopping rules can change learning curves. No optimal sample-size claim follows. |
| A four-times-batch uncertainty shortlist balances screening and diversity. | A fixed heuristic multiplier, retained from the original experiment. There is no fitted acquisition weight and no empirical claim that four is optimal. | Selector tests, tie rules and unchanged historical acquisitions. | Screening can discard observations that diversity-only would choose. The ablation suggests policy dependence, not the cause of any loss. |
| Tree spread can rank acquisition candidates. | Disagreement is computable from the forest. Broader benchmarks show that uncertainty usefulness varies by task ([Greenman et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012639)). | Ranking implementation and spread/error rank associations; no interval coverage test. | A high score can reflect unfamiliarity without useful learning; a low score can accompany large error. Do not interpret it as a probability. |
| Four paired seeds describe computational sensitivity. | Sharing initial sets supports direct within-seed comparisons; fixed seeds make them reproducible. | Paired histories and sample SD with `ddof=1`. | They are not independent biological replicates, a confidence interval, or a significance analysis. Evaluation outcomes are shared. |
| Pure uncertainty and pure diversity isolate components under the fixed protocol. | Removing one step creates a useful controlled comparison, not a proof of causal biological mechanism. | Policies share data, starts, budgets and forest; pure diversity receives no model score. | Interaction effects and representation limitations prevent attributing all performance differences to a single biological cause. |
| Evaluation outcomes are unavailable to acquisition code. | Necessary for the simulated label-budget question. Public availability still permits human knowledge. | Counterfactual tests change evaluation/unrevealed labels and require unchanged acquisition histories. | These checks establish behavior of this code path, not secure concealment or a fresh blind evaluation. |
| The recorded reuse is exploratory. | Original outcomes were public before the ablation. The fixed plan documents the new question without erasing prior knowledge. | Historical outputs and plan hashes are retained. | Further tuning on these observations cannot be presented as independent confirmation. |
| Numerical agreement is sufficient for reproduction of these results. | Finite-precision calculations can differ slightly across environments. | Exact input/identity checks and declared numeric tolerances; independent final-metric reconstruction. | Agreement does not prove unique model provenance, biological validity, or prospective usefulness. |

The [glossary](glossary.md) explains terminology. The [reference audit](reference-audit.json) separates literature support from the project’s own assumptions.
